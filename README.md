# ProductSignal

ProductSignal is an AI-assisted product discovery workspace that helps Product Managers turn customer feedback into evidence-backed product opportunities and prioritization decisions.

## Status

Early MVP in development.

## Product idea

ProductSignal is designed to help PMs move from raw customer feedback to better product decisions without handing the decision-making over to AI.

The workflow is:

1. Collect customer feedback
2. Identify recurring problems and themes
3. Review the evidence behind each insight
4. Turn validated problems into product opportunities
5. Prioritize opportunities using customer impact, frequency, business value, effort, and strategic alignment
6. Decide what to investigate, build, or leave alone

The product is intentionally designed around the idea that AI should assist product judgment rather than replace it.
